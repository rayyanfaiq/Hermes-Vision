Hermes Vision

Multi-Sensor Integration in Smart Glasses for Real-Time Driver Drowsiness Tracking and Adaptive AI-Based Alerting


Project Overview

Hermes Vision is a wearable driver-safety device designed to detect signs of driver drowsiness in real time. Instead of relying on a single metric, it combines computer vision, motion sensing, and physiological measurements to assess the driver's state and trigger appropriate warnings before fatigue contributes to an accident.

Hardware & Sensors

Inward-Facing Camera

Powered by an ESP32-S3 module with an OV3660 sensor. Monitors the driver's eyes and face (PERCLOS, blink frequency, gaze direction).

MAX30102 Sensor

An optical pulse oximeter providing contextual physiological measurements:

  • Heart Rate
  • Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO₂)

MPU6050 Motion Sensor

A 3-axis accelerometer and gyroscope combo. Used to measure subtle head orientation changes and potential head-dropping events indicative of microsleeps.

Power & Processing

Features a rechargeable battery, TP4056 charging module, and TPS63020 voltage regulator. Heavy computational tasks and emergency mapping are securely offloaded to a companion smartphone.

How The System Works

1. Data Collection

The inward camera, MAX30102, and MPU6050 simultaneously gather visual, physiological, and motion data from the driver.

2. Visual Processing

Analyzes blink duration and the proportion of time the eyes are substantially closed to calculate drowsiness indicators.

3. Gaze Calibration

Maps the driver's gaze to accepted zones: a forward road-view zone, and left/right mirror zones.

4. Multisensor Fusion

Combines visual eye-closure data with physical head-drop metrics to provide a highly reliable drowsiness assessment.

5. Alerts

Data is passed to a companion smartphone for heavy computation, which triggers adaptive, timely warnings to the driver.
